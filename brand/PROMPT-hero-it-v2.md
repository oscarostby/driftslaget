# Hovedbilde, versjon 2

Laget med det innebygde bildegenereringsverktøyet. Original: `hero-it-v2-original.png`. Optimalisert nettstedfil: `dist/assets/hero-it-v2.webp`.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: main hero photography for Driftslaget, a Norwegian small-business IT support and managed IT company. Create a refined realistic editorial photo that instantly reads as professional IT support, not generic business consulting. Wide landscape 3:2. A real small-business IT workspace in a modern Norwegian office: foreground a business laptop open facing viewer at a three-quarter angle with a clearly visible generic system-management interface composed of neat blue and green panels, next to a large external monitor showing a generic settings window with recognizable window-like visual hierarchy but no readable words. A compact network switch with neatly connected ethernet cables and small wireless access point visibly on the oak desk. To the right in background a small organized wall-mounted network cabinet. A technician in a plain dark forest-green overshirt is seated at right, seen mostly from shoulder and hands naturally working on the laptop, focus on equipment and technical work rather than face. Scandinavian architectural daylight, warm ivory office walls, pale oak, restrained plants, practical authentic workspace. Rich natural dark-green and slate-blue tones, professional high-end commercial photography, realistic ports and orderly cabling. Frame important screens and network hardware centrally so portrait crop still clearly communicates IT. No handshake, no lifestyle meeting, no server farm, no gamer RGB, no holograms, no artificial sci-fi graphics, no brand marks, no text overlay, no watermark. All people illustrative, not actual founders.
