## Logo — logo-original.png

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production logo symbol for Norwegian B2B IT support company named Driftslaget. Create ONE exceptionally clean flat geometric abstract emblem only, no lettering or words. Three thick connected ascending rounded ribbon blocks form a compact clever abstract lowercase d / three teammates in a forward-moving interlocking shape. The identity should feel Scandinavian, confident, friendly, solid, modern and distinctive, appropriate for reliable business IT. Single solid very dark forest green #123d31, with one small lime #d2ef78 accent block if composition benefits. Strong silhouette and simple geometry, balanced square icon, generous transparent padding. Flat vector-like sharp edges, no gradient, no 3D, no shadow, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no background. Actual transparent background.
